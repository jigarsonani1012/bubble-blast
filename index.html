Quick Guide
How to Play
Aim with your mouse or finger, line up matching colors, and keep the stack below the warning line.
Aim

Move to target

Slide your cursor or finger to steer the shot line. The bubble fires where you point.

Match

Pop 3 or more

Match the fired bubble with the same color to clear groups and earn points.

Bonus

Drop loose bubbles

When a cluster loses support, it falls for extra score and satisfying chain reactions.

Pressure

Watch the line

New rows push from the top. If the bubbles cross the dashed warning line, the run ends.
